
### problem and users
  University students often need to check different course syllabi to find important academic dates such as exams, quizzes, assignments, and projects. Since this information is spread across multiple documents, students spend extra time searching for important dates and may miss important deadlines or updates. Assessment dates may also change during the semester, making it difficult for students to stay informed and organize their study schedules. Instructors may also face difficulties coordinating assessment dates with other courses, while the academic department may find it challenging to organize official examination schedules and reduce scheduling conflicts across the university.The ASACS system provides one personalized academic calendar that includes  important dates for all the student's courses. This makes it easier for students to stay organized, manage their academic schedules, keep track of important academic events, and reduce the chance of missing important deadlines.
