
### problem and users
#### problem :
  students often open different course syllabi to check important academic dates for example : exams, quizzes, assignments, and other important events .
 This takes time and makes it easy to miss important deadlines .
  
   The ASACS system provides one simple calendar that includes all important dates for the student's courses. This makes it easier for students to stay organized and keep up with their courses also reduce the chance of missing deadlines
